# Backlog

Ideas and reports taken from the comments on the original "Ancient" Buildings page (SyndicateGamingNetwork, item 2566355159,
August 2021 to November 2024), read on 2026-10-08. Nothing here is promised. Each line says what was asked, what the original
author answered, and where it stands for this port. The first thing to do with an idea is to decide whether it belongs in this mod:
the mod's rule is to port without rebalancing, so anything that changes what the original does is a separate decision.

Status values: `open` (not decided), `declined` (decided against, reason given), `done` (already in the port), `answered` (a question
the comments settle).
